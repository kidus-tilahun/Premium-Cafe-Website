1. RADICAL WHITESPACE (BREATHING ROOM)
- Increase the vertical padding on all major sections drastically (use `py-32` or `py-40` in Tailwind).

2. EDITORIAL TYPOGRAPHY & SCALE
- The typography scale is too uniform. Make the main section headers much larger and more elegant. Use a high-end serif font like `Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond` with thin, tracking-wide settings (`tracking-wide font-light`).

3. ASYMMETRY & LAYOUT FLUIDITY
- Break the perfectly symmetrical grids. For the photo and menu sections, use an alternating, asymmetric editorial layout (e.g., a large image taking up 7 columns, and a minimal text block offset in the remaining columns with ample whitespace around it).

4. PREMIUM COLOR DEPTH
- Instead of harsh pure black (#000000) or flat gray, use a layered, rich dark palette. Use an incredibly deep charcoal-obsidian blend for backgrounds (`bg-[#121212]` or `bg-[#0D0D0D]`).
- For card containers or subtle shifts, use a translucent glassmorphism or a microscopic border (`border-white/[0.06] bg-white/[0.02] backdrop-blur-md`).

- Apply a subtle overlay mask or a light mix-blend-mode to image containers so that the imagery perfectly coordinates with the deep background colors. Images should have sharp, precise aspect ratios (like an elegant `aspect-[3/4]` or `aspect-[16/10]`).
